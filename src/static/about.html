About Mergington High School

Discovering opportunities beyond the classroom

Our mission

Mergington High School is committed to helping every student grow academically, socially, and creatively. We believe extracurricular activities are a powerful way to build confidence, leadership, and community.

Why club participation matters

Students who participate in clubs and teams gain practical experience, make lasting friendships, and discover new passions. Whether they are solving problems in math, creating art, or learning teamwork on the field, every activity supports well-rounded growth.

What we offer

  • Academic clubs and competitions
  • Creative and performing arts programs
  • Sports and wellness opportunities
  • Service and leadership experiences
